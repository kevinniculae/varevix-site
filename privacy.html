Privacy

Dataset Drift Preflight performs comparisons locally on CSV files that you explicitly select. The plugin does not upload CSV contents to Varevix servers and does not include product telemetry or user accounts.

The software displays local comparison results in IntelliJ IDEA. Files are not modified by the comparison action.

If you email us for support, your email service and our email provider process the correspondence. We use messages to respond to requests and manage support communications.

JetBrains Marketplace and your IDE are separate services, governed by their respective privacy information.

Contact: varevix.support@gmail.com

This privacy notice applies to Dataset Drift Preflight v0.3.0-beta, as currently released. It will be reviewed and updated if the plugin's functionality, data processing, or external integrations change.
